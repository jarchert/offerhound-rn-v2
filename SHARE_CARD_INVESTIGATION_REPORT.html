Changes

1. ShareRoleCardDialog

Added flexGrow: 1 and paddingBottom: spacing.xl + spacing.md (48pt) to the single ScrollView's content container. The sheet remains capped at 85%, while its card and Share/Copy action row remain in the same scrollable region.

content: {
  padding: spacing.lg,
  flexGrow: 1,
  gap: spacing.lg,
  paddingBottom: spacing.xl + spacing.md,
}

Coverage runs the component for all three real roles: scout, club_coach, and hs_coach. It verifies one ScrollView, Share and Copy Link descendants inside it, and the style invariants. Reverting the product change caused 3 of 9 tests to fail; restoring it returned 9 of 9.

2. SocialLinksManager inline athlete card

No product-code change was warranted. The actual chain is:

DashboardScreen outer ScrollView
  -> renderProfileTab()
    -> SocialLinksManager
      -> ProfileCardGenerator (including radar and its action bar)
      -> section-level CardShareActions

There are no nested vertical scroll containers, no fixed-height ancestor, and no ancestor combining overflow:hidden with a height constraint. The existing Card's rounded-corner overflow is unconstrained and does not clip normal-flow children. Four regression tests lock in these facts using a 1200pt tall card stub.

3. SharePlayerCardDialog tall-content variant

The original cd10343 implementation remains correct: ProfileCardGenerator is a direct child of the capture View, inside DialogContent's single ScrollView. A new stress test uses a 2000pt tall stub representing populated bio/social/radar/QR content and verifies top, middle, and bottom sentinels all remain descendants of the one ScrollView, with no nested vertical scroll or fixed-point clipping ancestor.
